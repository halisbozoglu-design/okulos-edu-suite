## Current authority lock
Current 2026 consolidated text of `Millî Eğitim Bakanlığı Yönetici ve Öğretmenlerinin Ders ve Ek Ders Saatlerine İlişkin Karar` Md.28 was recovered from the canonical legal-source artifacts used by this project. Md.28 explicitly refers to the MEB–YÖK coordination/cooperation protocol and the current teacher-practice directive.

### Md.28 operationally relevant locks
- Md.28/1 establishes the teacher-practice assignment family and points to the protocol + directive.
- Md.28/2 recognizes duties concerning implementation and entering student evaluations into the Uygulama Öğrencisi Değerlendirme Sistemi; communication, coordination, guidance and consultancy are included.
- Md.28/2 counts weekly 4 hours for the MEM application coordinator, 2 hours for the application-school principal and 2 hours for the application-school coordinator as extra-course duty.
- Md.28/3 sets application-teacher extra-course hours by course family and caps the aggregate at 10 hours per week.
- Md.28/4 requires a separate MEM application coordinator for each university.
- Md.28/4 requires one of the deputy principals in each application school to be appointed application-school coordinator.
- Md.28/4 requires application schools and the number of assigned administrators/teachers by province, district, school and branch to be redetermined each academic year under the directive.

Md.28 is an assignment/payment/coordination authority. It does not replace the detailed operational directive. Therefore it cannot by itself prove the 6/2 threshold, orientation meeting, activity planning, result-delivery or discipline-notification legs in HB-1788..HB-1794.

## Row decisions
### HB-1787
Current Md.28/4 is a very strong semantic/current-text match, and adds the legally material qualification that the application-school coordinator is one of the deputy principals. Promotion is deferred until live official source + exact row-ledger/dedupe lock.

### HB-1788
The master contains two numeric limits: max 6 application students per application teacher and max 2 students per lesson. Those values are not proven by Md.28. Exact current directive clause is mandatory.

### HB-1789
School-unit orientation and information about activities are operational coordinator duties. Md.28's generic coordination/guidance language is adjacent support, not exact proof.

### HB-1790
A specific meeting plus notification of duties/responsibilities is a concrete event. Generic communication/coordination authority is insufficient.

### HB-1791
Planning application activities jointly with the application faculty member and application teacher requires an exact actor/action directive clause.

### HB-1792
The master combines monitoring, evaluation, guidance and preventive/corrective action without naming the actor. It must be atomized and actor-bound before promotion.

### HB-1793
Must split into at least: (1) system entry, (2) evaluation, (3) delivery of application results to the application-school coordinator. Md.28/2 supports the system/evaluation family but does not prove all three legs as one parent.

### HB-1794
Must split into at least: (1) attendance compliance, (2) daily lesson schedule compliance, (3) curriculum/teaching-program compliance, (4) disciplinary-event notification to the relevant faculty/higher school. No compound parent promotion.
