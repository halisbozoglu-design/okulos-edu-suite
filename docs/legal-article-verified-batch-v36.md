## Withheld
- HB-2204 — master title says “Mesleki Açık Öğretim İmam Hatip Lisesi Yüz Yüze Eğitim Denklik Komisyonu (İHL)”. Current 2024 Open Education Regulation Md25/2-a explicitly creates a school/institution commission only for MAÖL students with journeyman/master certificates for course exemption/equivalence. The title/scope is not one-to-one; do not promote.
- HB-2205 — master title “Alan/Dal Kontenjan Belirleme Komisyonu (MTAL)” does not exactly match the current OOKY Md25 legal organ, which is the general preparation/9th-grade quota determination commission. Area/branch wording appears in MESEM quota rules but does not create a separately named commission. Withhold pending split/rename.
- HB-2206 — current rules support coordinator-teacher assignment but an exact named “Mesleki Eğitim/Koordinatör Öğretmen Görevlendirilmesi Belirleme Komisyonu” clause was not established. Withhold.
- HB-2210 — talent exam commission is guide/calendar sensitive; exact current guide binding required before promotion.
