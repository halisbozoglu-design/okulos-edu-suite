### HB-1776 — Belgenet/EBYS
WITHHELD. `Belgenet` is an implementation/product name, not legal authority. Legal executable children must be based on electronic official-correspondence rules and the institution's applicable EBYS configuration.

### HB-1777 — petition OR information-access application
WITHHELD compound parent. It must split into distinct statutory deadline children:
1. `PETITION_3071_ART7`: petition result or process status -> reasoned response within 30 days; if status is reported while processing continues, result is additionally notified.
2. `INFO_ACCESS_4982_ART11_BASE`: access within 15 business days.
3. `INFO_ACCESS_4982_ART11_EXTENDED`: access within 30 business days only when statutory cross-unit/cross-institution/multi-institution conditions exist; extension and reason must be notified in writing before the first 15-business-day period expires.
4. `INFO_ACCESS_4982_ART12_RESPONSE`: answer electronically or in writing; rejection includes reason and remedies.
Official RG for 4982: https://resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2003/10/20031024.htm
3071 current-law family remains a separate authority and cannot inherit 4982 timing.

### HB-1778 — upper-authority information-access routing
WITHHELD. `Upper authority sent it` does not itself determine the statutory clock, responsible actor, or route. Exact child requires originating authority, application type, receipt/transfer event and applicable statutory provision.

### HB-1779 — official correspondence regulation compliance
WITHHELD master parent pending row-ledger/dedupe binding. Current authority identity is locked as `Resmî Yazışmalarda Uygulanacak Usul ve Esaslar Hakkında Yönetmelik` (CB Decision 2646). Generic compliance cannot be promoted merely by identifying the correct regulation title.

### HB-1780 — confidentiality
WITHHELD compound parent; current special authority is locked to `Gizlilik Dereceli Belgelerde Uygulanacak Usul ve Esaslar Hakkında Yönetmelik`, CB Decision 5529, RG 26.04.2022/31821. The regulation covers public institutions and separates Çok Gizli, Gizli and Hizmete Özel handling, including preparation, sending, receipt, storage, copying and declassification. Generic secrecy action must therefore resolve classification first.
Official publication identity: 26.04.2022, RG 31821, Decision 5529.

### HB-1781 — ASLI GİBİDİR
WITHHELD generic parent; exact conditional child locked. Current official-correspondence Regulation Art.29/2 permits certified physical-copy generation in specified exceptional/legacy physical-document circumstances. `ASLI GİBİDİR`, certifying official identity/title/date/signature requirements match the master semantics, but the master parent omits the applicability condition. Never turn this into a universal rule for every copied document.

### HB-1782 — electronic user groups/authorizations
WITHHELD. Requires exact MEB/system role authority. School-principal status plus existence of an electronic system does not prove every authorization action.

### HB-1783 — information-system error/irregularity controls
WITHHELD compound parent. Prevention, detection, correction, access control, logging and incident response are separate controls and require their own current security authority/role mapping.

### HB-1784 — MEBBİS timely/correct/current data
WITHHELD generic parent. Each MEBBİS module has its own actor, field, source record, deadline and validation rule. `MEBBİS` alone is not a legal source.

### HB-1785 — teacher application approval/rejection
WITHHELD. Appointment, examination, lodging and in-service training applications must be modeled by application type; one generic school-principal approval rule cannot inherit all special procedures.

### HB-1786 — three physical copies
WITHHELD legacy-looking parent. No universal current authority was locked requiring every approved electronic application to be printed in three copies for teacher + school file + district MEM. Preserve only if an application-specific current authority independently requires it.

## Deadline separation lock
Do not collapse these clocks:
- Official-correspondence Regulation Art.33: requested **document** -> 5 business days; requested **information/opinion** -> 15 business days.
- 3071 Art.7 petition -> 30 days.
- 4982 Art.11 information access -> 15 business days; conditional 30 business days + advance written extension notice.
- A special process may impose a different deadline and overrides the generic routing rule for that child.
