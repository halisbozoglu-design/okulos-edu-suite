## Exact boundary
Section `2.17 OKUL/KURUM ÇEVRE İLİŞKİSİ` is exactly HB-1832..HB-1837. HB-1838 starts `3.1 ÖĞRETMENLER KURULU`.

- HB-1832: education activities value family/environment relations and cooperation.
- HB-1833: school administrators promote school activities.
- HB-1834: at least one parent meeting per term to inform parents about work.
- HB-1835: cooperation with nearby public/private/industry/trade organizations.
- HB-1836: institution-workplace relations developed and reflected in practice.
- HB-1837: students guided for instructional activities/employment/planning through cooperation with universities, NGOs, sector businesses, professional chambers and professionals.

## Current authority findings
1. OÖKY Md19/1 directly supports HB-1832: school, family and environment relations/cooperation are emphasized in education activities.
2. OÖKY Md19/2 directly supports HB-1833: school administrators promote school activities and inform teachers, students, parents and environment about work.
3. Current 2026 MEB inspection guidance audits school-environment relations through OÖKY Md19 and Md215; it does not itself establish `at least once per term` parent-meeting frequency.
4. Therefore HB-1834 remains WITHHELD. Generic parent cooperation does not prove a recurring per-term deadline.
5. The handbook/checklist repeats `at least one parent meeting per term`, but checklist text is not standalone legal authority.
6. HB-1835 is broader than OÖKY Md19. Cooperation with public/private/industry/trade organizations may be appropriate operationally, but exact universal duty is not proven by Md19 alone.
7. HB-1836 is workplace-specific and therefore must be conditioned to vocational/enterprise-training applicability; it must not inherit `ALL` school scope.
8. HB-1837 text matches vocational-program framework language requiring/guiding cooperation with universities, NGOs, professional chambers, sector firms and professionals for instructional/employment/planning aims. It is program/field/version bound, not a universal general-school rule.
9. Current MEB 2026 MESEM inspection guidance separately audits enterprise vocational education, workplace suitability, contracts, coordinators, OSH and related OÖKY provisions, confirming workplace relations are a specialized vocational authority family rather than a generic school-community clause.
