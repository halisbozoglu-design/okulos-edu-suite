## Exact legal/model decisions
### HB-1826 — Okul Sağlığı Yönetim Ekibi
Material mismatch. The canonical master omits a student member. Current official Health Ministry material describes the team as one administrator, one teacher, one student and one school-family-association member; where available, guidance counselor and health worker are natural members. Parent remains WITHHELD. Executable current child must be version-bound to the applicable school-health protocol/guide.

### HB-1827 — Okula özgü Okul Sağlığı Planı
Strong program-rule match. The applicable guide requires a school-specific annual plan prepared by the school-health team. However, the 18.04.2025 MEB–MoH protocol explicitly initiates updating of the older application guide. Rule remains VERSION_BOUND / WITHHELD at master-parent count level until the applicable replacement/current guide version is locked.

### HB-1828 — annual family-physician examination/follow-up
Strong current support. The 2025 Aile Hekimliği Rehberi continues annual periodic examination/follow-up for school-age children under school-health cooperation arrangements. Responsibility belongs primarily to the health-service/family-physician system; the school workflow should facilitate/coordinate where required, not pretend to perform the medical examination. Parent is not promoted without exact current clause + row dedupe/live source lock.

### HB-1829 — numerical examination/vaccination/screening data
SPLIT REQUIRED. At least three layers are distinct:
1. source clinical/individual health record held by authorized health actors;
2. authorized reporting/transfer from health authority;
3. school-level aggregate/statistical monitoring where lawfully supplied/required.
The school must never infer a right to collect/store raw individual health data from a generic requirement to keep numerical statistics. Health data is special-category personal data; access, purpose, minimization, retention and role-based visibility must be separately resolved.

### HB-1830 — school-health file
Program-document construct, not a license for unrestricted health-data storage. The file/document set must be generated from the applicable protocol/guide version and must follow document-retention + privacy rules. Where only aggregate/status evidence is needed, raw medical records are not copied into the school-health file.

### HB-1831 — Covid-19 measures
Legacy emergency rule. The 2021 pandemic-era school measures are not converted into an always-on 2026 recurring legal task. A pandemic/infectious-disease task is activated only by a current competent public-health authority trigger, circular, protocol or emergency measure. Historical completed Covid tasks remain immutable evidence of the period in which they applied.
