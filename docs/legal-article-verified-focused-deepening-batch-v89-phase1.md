Title: `Öğretmenlerin Sınıf İçi Etkinlikleri ve Öğretim Faaliyetlerinin İzlenmesi, Değerlendirilmesi ve Geliştirilmesine İlişkin Yönerge`.

### 2. TKB Denetim Rehberleri ve Denetim Esasları

### HB-1795 — WITHHELD / LEGACY-PROVENANCE

- The current TKB Denetim Esasları and the current teacher monitoring directive do not establish a universal school duty to maintain a `teftiş/denetim defteri`.
- Current processes are report/MEBBİS/document-system oriented.
- No ARTICLE_VERIFIED promotion.

### HB-1796 — WITHHELD / STORAGE-RULE-SPLIT
- Current inspection authority proves that reports are produced/sent and followed.
- `Special file` is not proven as the universal current storage mechanism.
- Retention/classification must resolve through current file plan/EBYS and any specific confidentiality rule.

### HB-1797 — STRONG CONDITIONAL CHILD / PARENT WITHHELD
- Current TKB 5.2.3 gives an exact operational child: actual inspection report received -> within one month -> development plan based on findings/solutions -> send to relevant il/ilçe MEM -> implementation monitored.
- Master parent omits trigger, one-month clock and MEM delivery.
- Parent stays withheld; exact child is canonical.

### HB-1798 — MATERIAL MASTER MISMATCH / NO ROLLBACK WITHOUT LEDGER
- Master says **each education term** at least once.
- Current directive says **within a school year** at least once.
- The current actor composition can include school principal + zümre president/advisor depending on circumstances, not always principal acting alone.
- Therefore HB-1798 cannot be ARTICLE_VERIFIED as written.
- No rollback is booked unless historical row-level ARTICLE_VERIFIED evidence is recovered.

### HB-1799 — PARTIAL AUTHORITY / WITHHELD
- OÖKY Md78 establishes the principal as primary education/teaching leader responsible for school management under law and regulations.
- Current inspection/monitoring framework supports supervision and follow-up semantics.
- Exact compound phrase `all personnel works + detected deficiencies + follow-up until cured` is not yet locked to one current clause.

### HB-1800 — WITHHELD / HANDBOOK-ONLY CURRENT EXPECTATION
- Current administrator handbook still lists annual brifing-file preparation/update.
- Older MEB document families also list a brifing file.
- No current higher-order exact legal provision was found establishing a universal annual brifing-file duty for all schools.
- Handbook checklist does not by itself become ARTICLE_VERIFIED.

### HB-1801 — CURRENT STRATEGIC-PLANNING CANDIDATE / WITHHELD
- 2024-2028 MEB strategic planning materials require systematic monitoring/evaluation and official school/organization guides use monitoring/evaluation team structures.
- Exact school-level compulsory team composition/actor clause remains to be locked from the current school/organization strategic-plan guide before promotion.

### HB-1802 — GENERIC DIGITAL-OVERSIGHT / WITHHELD
- Principal has managerial responsibility and electronic systems impose module-specific duties.
- There is no single current clause proving that every electronic process is subject to the exact generic master wording.
- Must split by e-Okul/MEBBİS/EBYS/module when enforcement or deadline is involved.
